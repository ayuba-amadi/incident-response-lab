# Task 1 — Incident Analysis

## Purpose

This directory contains the explanatory analytical documents
supporting the initial ransomware incident assessment.

The documents should be read together rather than interpreted
as independent findings. Each addresses a different aspect of
the same fictional Northbridge Skills Institute scenario.

## Analysis documents

### 1. Scenario Context and Impact

**File:** [scenario-context-and-impact.md](scenario-context-and-impact.md)

Introduces the fictional Northbridge Skills Institute environment, affected services, operational dependencies and examination constraints.

### 2. Incident Chronology

**File:** [incident-chronology.md](incident-chronology.md)

Organises the reported events into a time-based incident record, preserving their evidence identifiers and temporal relationships.

### 3. Observations, Hypotheses and Questions

**File:** [observations-hypotheses-questions.md](observations-hypotheses-questions.md)

Separates reported observations from possible explanations and unanswered investigation questions.

### 4. Severity Classification

**File:** [severity-classification.md](severity-classification.md)

Documents the initial provisional High severity assessment and its supporting rationale.

### 5. Severity Reassessment Triggers

**File:** [severity-reassessment-triggers.md](severity-reassessment-triggers.md)

Identifies developments that could justify changing the initial incident severity classification.

### 6. Confidentiality, Integrity and Availability

**File:** [cia-impact-assessment.md](cia-impact-assessment.md)

Assesses the potential security and business consequences across the confidentiality, integrity and availability dimensions.

### 7. Response Prioritisation

**File:** [response-prioritisation.md](response-prioritisation.md)

Explains the proposed order of response activities and their relationship to operational and evidentiary priorities.

## Recommended reading sequence

Start with the scenario context and chronology, then review
the distinction between observations and hypotheses.
Next, examine the severity classification, reassessment
triggers, CIA impact and response priorities.

## Methodological safeguards

- An event's position in the chronology does not prove causation.
- Investigative hypotheses are not established forensic findings.
- Potential exposure is not equivalent to confirmed disclosure.
- The initial High severity rating remains provisional.
- Proposed priorities do not establish that actions occurred.
