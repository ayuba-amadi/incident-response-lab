# Task 2 — Incident Response Policy

## Purpose

This directory contains the proposed incident response policy
for the Northbridge Skills Institute ransomware scenario.

The policy provides the governance foundation for recognising,
reporting, assessing and coordinating a significant cybersecurity
incident.

It is an academic policy proposal, not a formally authorised
or operationally implemented institutional policy.

## Policy document

### Incident Response Policy

[Read the complete proposed policy](incident-response-policy.md)

The document establishes the policy-level framework for incident
management and the relationship between operational staff,
incident leadership, management oversight and continuity needs.

## How the policy should be read

The policy is a governance document. The detailed workflows and
assignment of responsibility are documented separately, rather
than embedded in the policy alone.

Readers should distinguish:

- Policy expectations from detailed operational procedures.
- Incident declaration from response activation.
- Normal decision authority from restricted emergency authority.
- Operational response from management risk acceptance.
- Proposed responsibilities from confirmed organisational appointments.
